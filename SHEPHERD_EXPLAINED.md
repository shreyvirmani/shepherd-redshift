## 1. What is SHEPHERD?

Most women's safety applications on the market today suffer from one fatal flaw: **they only react after an assault or dangerous encounter has already happened.** They rely on the victim being able to physically grab their phone, unlock it, open an application, and hold down a panic button while fighting off an attacker. In real-world ambushes or high-stress situations, this "conscious action" model fails over 80% of the time.

**SHEPHERD** is built on an entirely different premise: **Proactive Intelligence**.

Instead of waiting for a disaster, SHEPHERD acts as an autonomous guardian:
1. **It monitors transit journeys** and automatically checks on the user if they stop moving, take an unexpected detour, or fail to reach their destination on time.
2. **It eliminates responder friction** by generating a web-based emergency link that friends, family, or police can open immediately without downloading any app or creating an account.
3. **It organizes evidence forensically** by calculating tamper-proof cryptographic signatures (digital fingerprints) for screenshots, threatening messages, and voice recordings so they can stand up in a court of law.
4. **It connects the dots** across repeat harassment events so victims can prove patterns of stalking or intimidation that law enforcement might otherwise dismiss as isolated incidents.

3. **After (Justice & Evidence)**: Securely catalog evidence with mathematical proof that it hasn't been altered, analyze recurring patterns, and export formal reports for attorneys and police.

## 3. The 7 Core Modules

### 3.1 Sentinel (Proactive Transit Guardian)
* **What it is**: Your personal safety co-pilot while walking home, taking a cab, or commuting alone.
* **How it works**:
  - You enter where you are going (destination), how you are traveling (walking, transit, driving), and your initial concern level.
  - As you travel, Sentinel monitors your progress along a **Safe Corridor**.
  - **Dead-Man's Switch**: If your phone stops moving in an isolated area, or if you take an abnormal detour, Sentinel doesn't panic—it initiates an empathetic full-screen verification check asking: *"Are you okay?"*
  - **Auto-Escalation**: You have a clear countdown timer to respond. If you tap *"Yes, I'm Safe"*, the journey continues normally. If you tap *"I Need Help"* or if you **fail to respond before the timer expires**, Sentinel automatically initiates distress mode and alerts your emergency circle.
  - **Hold-to-Confirm Silent Assist**: Features a 1200ms hold-to-activate button with progressive haptic vibration. This prevents accidental triggers while ensuring you can activate help silently inside a pocket or bag without alarming an aggressor.

### 3.2 SafeCircle (Trusted Network Check-ins)
* **What it is**: A coordinated check-in system for your trusted friends, roommates, or family members.
* **How it works**:
  - You designate trusted contacts with their phone numbers and relationship (e.g., Mom, Sister, Best Friend).
  - You can schedule a **Safety Session** with an expected deadline (e.g., *"Heading to a dinner date, should be home by 11:00 PM"*).
  - Before the deadline hits, you tap a button to check in as safe.
  - If the deadline passes without your check-in, SHEPHERD automatically alerts your chosen contacts with your last known details.

### 3.3 Rescue Relay (Zero-App Emergency Dispatch)
* **What it is**: The emergency portal sent to contacts or responders when an alert is triggered.
* **The Problem It Solves**: In typical safety apps, when you hit SOS, your contacts only receive a notification if they *also* downloaded the app, remembered their password, and allowed notifications. That friction costs lives.
* **How Rescue Relay Works**:
  - When an alert triggers, SHEPHERD dispatches an SMS or WhatsApp message containing a secure, tokenized web link (e.g., `shepherd.app/relay/sec_tok_99182`).
  - Anyone clicking the link sees a **live emergency dashboard** in their mobile browser—**no app download, no login required**.
  - It displays:
    1. Who triggered the alert and the exact timestamp.
    2. An acknowledgement button: *"Confirm you have seen this alert"* so others know help is on the way.
    3. Last recorded GPS coordinates on an interactive map.
    4. Current battery percentage, speed, and heading.
    5. A one-tap button opening Google Maps with **turn-by-turn directions** to the user's location.
    6. Direct buttons to call the user, call 112/police, or forward the link via WhatsApp.

### 3.4 EvidenceVault (Cryptographic Proof Storage)
* **What it is**: A private digital safety deposit box for storing evidence of harassment, abuse, or stalking.
* **How it works**:
  - Upload screenshots of harassing messages, call logs, audio clips, or photos.
  - The instant a file is uploaded, SHEPHERD runs a mathematical formula called **SHA-256** to generate a unique 64-character digital fingerprint of the file.
  - Even if someone modifies a single pixel or comma, the fingerprint completely changes.
  - **Verification**: At any point, clicking *"Verify Hash"* re-calculates the fingerprint and proves that the stored file is 100% identical to the day it was uploaded.
  - This establishes a clean, tamper-proof **chain of custody** that lawyers and judges look for.

### 3.5 ThreatLens (AI Incident Intelligence)
* **What it is**: An AI analysis engine that reads incident descriptions and screenshots to help categorize what is happening.
* **How it works**:
  - Uses Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to extract text directly from uploaded screenshots of chat messages or emails.
  - Analyzes the language against an established **harassment taxonomy** (e.g., Stalking, Verbal Abuse, Coercion, Physical Intimidation).
  - Highlights specific indicators (such as repeated unwanted contact or threats of retaliation).
  - **Ethical AI Rule**: ThreatLens explicitly states its limitations. It does **not** make legal judgments or claim infallible truth; it provides an objective, structured summary to assist the victim in articulating their experience.

### 3.6 PatternGraph (Visual Relationship Detective)
* **What it is**: An interactive network graph that visually connects different incidents to uncover patterns.
* **Why it matters**: Stalkers and repeat harassers often rely on fragmentation. They send a message today, show up outside an office two weeks later, and call from an unknown number a month later. Individually, each event seems small; together, they constitute a pattern of criminal harassment.
* **How it works**:
  - Displays incidents as interactive visual nodes on a canvas.
  - Draws connections between them based on:
    - **User-reported links**: Connections you explicitly noted (e.g., *"Same person who followed me last Tuesday"*).
    - **Suggested similarities**: Algorithmic suggestions pointing out identical phone numbers, shared vehicle descriptions, or matching locations.
  - Gives the user a visual story they can present to investigators.

### 3.7 JusticeBridge (Court-Ready Dossier Generator)
* **What it is**: A one-click tool that compiles everything into a formal, structured PDF legal report.
* **What the report contains**:
  - Exact chronological timeline of incidents.
  - Detailed incident descriptions and user-provided locations.
  - Index of all evidence files with their original filenames, upload dates, and SHA-256 verification hashes.
  - Transparent AI disclosures stating how ThreatLens assisted in organizing the data.
  - Space for official signatures, case numbers, and police station filing stamps.

## 4. Dictionary of Important Terms & Concepts

| Term | What It Means (Simple Explanation) | Real-World Usage in SHEPHERD |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sentinel Session** | An active tracking period when SHEPHERD monitors your journey from origin to destination. | *"Starting a Sentinel session before walking back from the library to the dorm."* |
| **Dead-Man's Switch** | A safety mechanism that triggers automatically if the user fails to confirm they are safe within a set time limit. | If you are incapacitated or your phone is dropped, the countdown expires and alerts your contacts automatically. |
| **Safe Corridor** | A designated geographical buffer zone (usually 100–300 meters) around an illuminated, safe route. | If your GPS location moves outside this corridor, Sentinel notes the deviation and prompts a safety check. |
| **Silent Assist** | A discreet panic trigger designed to be activated without looking at the screen or making noise. | Uses a 1.2-second hold with haptic vibrations so you can confirm it activated in your pocket without an attacker knowing. |
| **Hold-to-Confirm** | Requiring the user to press and hold a button rather than clicking it once. | Prevents accidental false alarms caused by bumping the phone inside a purse. |
| **Concern Level** | A 4-tier indicator of your comfort level during travel: `Normal`, `Advisory`, `Warning`, `Critical`. | Adjusts the frequency of automated checks and changes the color of the safety corridor on the map. |
| **Rescue Relay** | A web-based, zero-friction emergency portal accessible via a secure link. | Sent to your emergency contacts via SMS/WhatsApp so they can view your live location without having an app installed. |
| **Relay Token** | A unique, temporary security key embedded in the Rescue Relay link. | Guarantees that only people who received your specific emergency link can view your distress location. |
| **SafeCircle Contact** | A trusted friend, guardian, or neighbor whose contact details you have authorized for safety check-ins. | Contacts receive automated SMS reminders if you fail to complete your journey or check-in on time. |
| **SHA-256 Hash** | A cryptographic formula that turns any file into a unique 64-character fingerprint (e.g., `e3b0c44298fc1c149...`). | Proves to a court that your screenshot or audio evidence was not tampered with, edited in Photoshop, or falsified. |
| **Chain of Custody** | A chronological paper/digital trail showing who collected, stored, and accessed evidence. | Ensures that police or courts cannot throw out your evidence on claims of tampering or contamination. |
| **OCR (Optical Character Recognition)** | Technology that extracts readable text from image files. | Reads screenshots of WhatsApp chats, Instagram DMs, or threatening text messages automatically. |
| **Harassment Taxonomy** | A standardized classification dictionary used to categorize different types of safety threats. | Categorizes incidents into clear legal categories like Stalking, Coercion, Harassment, or Physical Threat. |
| **PatternGraph Node** | A visual circle representing one recorded safety incident on the relationship map. | Clicking a node highlights all other incidents related to it. |
| **Review Priority Score** | An objective numerical score (0 to 100) indicating how urgently an incident record requires human review. | Calculated from factors like weapon presence, repeated perpetrator contact, or escalations over time. |
| **Zero-Knowledge Drafts** | Incident reports and notes that are saved exclusively on your phone until you choose to upload them. | If you lose internet or want to delete a draft before uploading, nothing is ever sent to or stored on our servers. |
| **Row-Level Security (RLS)** | A database security rule ensuring that only you can read or edit your own records. | Guarantees that no other user on SHEPHERD can ever access your incidents, evidence, or contacts. |

## 5. How It Works in Real Life (3 Concrete Scenarios)

### Scenario A: Walking Home Alone at Night
> **The User**: Priya, a college student, leaving a study session at 10:45 PM to walk 1.5 km to her apartment.

1. **Before Leaving**: Priya opens SHEPHERD, goes to **Sentinel**, and enters her dorm as the destination. She selects *"Walking"* and taps **"Start Sentinel Journey"**.
2. **During the Walk**: 
   - Sentinel displays her route on a live map, surrounded by an illuminated boulevard corridor.
   - Her telemetry shows: `ETA: 11:05 PM`, `Battery: 68%`, `GPS: Active (pulsing green dot)`.
   - Halfway home, an unfamiliar car stops near her and lingers. Priya feels unsafe.
3. **Discreet Action**: Without taking her phone out of her jacket pocket, she holds down the **Silent Assist** button. After 1.2 seconds, she feels three distinct haptic buzzes confirming the alert triggered.
4. **Instant Rescue**:
   - Her sister and roommate receive an immediate WhatsApp alert: *"🛡️ SHEPHERD Alert: Priya triggered a safety alert at 10:57 PM. Location: Main Street & 4th Ave."*
   - Her sister taps the link, instantly sees Priya's exact location, and taps **"Directions"** to navigate directly to her while dialing her phone.

---

### Scenario B: Documenting Workplace Stalking Over Time
> **The User**: Sarah, a corporate professional, receiving anonymous threatening notes and repeated unwanted encounters from a colleague.

1. **Documenting Evidence**: Sarah creates an incident whenever an encounter occurs. She uploads photos of the notes and screenshots of blocked calls.
2. **Cryptographic Protection**: SHEPHERD immediately hashes every file with SHA-256. If Sarah's phone is ever lost or broken, the cloud vault preserves the immutable proof.
3. **Pattern Analysis**:
   - In **PatternGraph**, Sarah notices three incidents occurring exactly at 6:30 PM in the company parking lot on Wednesdays.
   - ThreatLens flags the phrase *"I saw what you were wearing today"* as persistent surveillance.
4. **Legal Action**: Sarah clicks **JusticeBridge** to generate a chronological PDF dossier. She presents this structured report to HR and local law enforcement, who approve an immediate restraining order due to the verifiable audit trail.

---

### Scenario C: Evening Check-in for an Unfamiliar Rideshare
> **The User**: Ananya, taking an interstate cab at night.

1. **SafeCircle Session**: Ananya starts a check-in session: *"Cab ride to airport, expected arrival 9:30 PM"*. She links her mother and roommate.
2. **Autonomous Monitoring**: If Ananya reaches the airport, she taps *"I'm Safe — Check in"*.
3. **What if something goes wrong?** If the cab diverts into an isolated route and the deadline passes without Ananya's check-in, SHEPHERD sends an urgent SMS notification to her mother with the cab's last known trajectory.

## 7. Important Safety & Ethical Boundaries

To maintain absolute trust and legal credibility, SHEPHERD adheres to strict ethical guardrails:

1. **No Fake Claims of Police Dispatch**: SHEPHERD never claims to possess a secret automated link to emergency services unless verified local agreements exist. It acts as an emergency coordination and information tool, directing users to call local emergency lines (e.g., 112 or 911) when in immediate danger.
2. **AI is Informational, Not Judicial**: ThreatLens classifications are clearly labeled as AI-assisted analysis. They provide helpful organization for victims, but never claim to replace legal counsel or forensic examiners.
3. **User Consent is Absolute**: No contact is ever notified, and no location is ever shared, without the user explicitly initiating a session or giving permission.
4. **Zero-Knowledge Privacy**: Your drafts remain on your personal device. If you delete a local draft, no copy exists on our servers.
5. **No Hallucinated Threat Scoring**: Risk scores are calculated using clear, transparent rules (e.g., presence of physical threats or repeated contact) rather than opaque "black-box" predictions.
